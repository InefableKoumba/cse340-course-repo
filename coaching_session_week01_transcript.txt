[COACH]:
That's completely fine and a great moment to learn together! 

Here is a simple breakdown:
1. **`grid-template-columns: 1fr;`**: On desktop screens, your grid items might sit side-by-side in multiple columns (like 2, 3, or 4 across). But on mobile screens (600px wide or narrower), multiple columns would get squished and unreadable. Setting `grid-template-columns: 1fr;` tells CSS to collapse the grid into **a single full-width column**, stacking each card cleanly on top of the other.
2. **`flex-direction: column`**: By default, Flexbox aligns items horizontally side-by-side (`row`). On narrow mobile screens, horizontal navbars can cause awkward overflow or text wrapping. Changing to `flex-direction: column` stacks the navigation elements vertically (one above another) with nice spacing.
